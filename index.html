ABOUT

I'm an engineer on ERCOT's Operation Analytics team, where I monitor the real-time performance of energy storage resources and tune the EMS systems — Load Frequency Control, the Resource Limit Calculator, and Generation-to-be-Dispatched — that keep grid frequency stable. I build Python tooling and apply statistical, probabilistic, and machine-learning methods to quantify ancillary services and sharpen reliability monitoring.

Texas A&M electrical engineering grad (3.9 GPA, minors in CS and math), with prior stints at EDP Renewables and Amazon. I like turning messy grid data into decisions — and I'm currently building open-source tooling around BESS verification and dispatch economics. Targeting energy-storage and grid roles in the Pacific Northwest, or remote.
